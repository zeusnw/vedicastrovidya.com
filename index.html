The Astrologer

Rooted in Authentic Jyotish

My journey into Vedic astrology began 15 years ago alongside my ShivYog sadhana — and over time I came to understand that Jyotish and ShivYog are two sides of the same divine coin.

Jyotish shows you the karmic map you brought into this lifetime — the strengths, the challenges, the planetary periods that govern your evolution. ShivYog gives you the tools to consciously navigate and transform those patterns. Together they offer a complete system for conscious living.

Every reading I offer is grounded in the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra — the foundational text of Vedic astrology — combined with decades of practical application across hundreds of charts.
